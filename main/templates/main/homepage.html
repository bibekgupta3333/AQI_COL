Air Quality Index (AQI)

An air quality index is used by government agencies to communicate to the public how polluted the air currently is or how polluted is the forecast to become. Public health risks increase as the AQI rises.

Machine Learning algorithm can predict AQI using various parameters such as meteorological factors, particulate matters (PM) etc. so that air quality can be kept on check.
